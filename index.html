ABOUT

Four friends meeting monthly since 2022 to discuss short stories. One person picks two stories, everyone reads, we talk for 75 minutes on Google Meet.

"Two stories, two different authors, hopefully two different time periods. I feel like we're reading good stuff and have good conversations. That's all I wanted out of the group." — JC, Jan 2023

What we like: older narrator reflecting on childhood · ambiguous endings · devastating final paragraphs · stories that reward re-reading · short enough to read twice · increasing bewilderment

"Lord, increase my bewilderment."
— Muslim prayer, via Fanny Howe, via Hannah
